Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your account details and the expense records you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide solely to operate Spendly: displaying your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and maintaining your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties. Where third-party services are used for core functionality, such as hosting or infrastructure, they are held to the same standards of confidentiality outlined in this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page.
